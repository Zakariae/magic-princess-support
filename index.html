Privacy policy

Effective 22 October 2026

Mousaab Khaldi provides Magic Princess: Dress Up for iOS. This policy explains how information is handled in version 1.2 of the game and when you contact us or visit this page.

Information stored by the game

The game stores your settings, progress, selected characters and outfits, unlocked content, purchase entitlements and temporary in-game access on your device. It also stores the artwork you create in the game’s local gallery, together with local file identifiers and creation times. A Unity component may create a local installation identifier. In this version, these local records are not uploaded to a server operated by us.

You do not need a game account. The game does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location, microphone access or camera access.

Saving artwork to Photos

If you choose to save a creation to your Photos library, the game asks iOS for permission to save that image. On iOS 14 and later it requests add-only access. Earlier supported iOS versions use the older Photos permission mechanism.

The game does not browse or import your existing photo library. You can change the game’s Photos permission in your device settings. Photos you save may be handled by iCloud Photos or other services according to your own settings.

In-app purchases

Apple processes in-app purchases through the App Store. The game receives product and transaction information, including purchase receipts, to unlock content and restore eligible purchases. It records the resulting entitlements locally.

We do not receive your payment-card details through the game and do not send its purchase receipts to a developer-operated verification server. Apple handles Apple Account, payment and purchase information under its own App Store privacy terms.

Advertising and analytics

This version has live advertising disabled. We do not use its bundled ad software to request or display ads. The current game does not enable developer analytics or send gameplay events to our servers.

The Unity purchasing and services components are used in the configuration described in this policy. This describes the current game version; other Unity services and future versions may have different practices.

Information you send for support

If you email Mousaab.khaldi@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use that information to respond to your request. Please avoid sending payment-card details or other unnecessary sensitive information.

We keep support messages while handling your request and delete them when they are no longer needed for that purpose, unless we need to keep them to resolve an ongoing dispute or meet a legal obligation. You can request deletion using the email address above.

This website

Opening this page is separate from playing the game. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not they are signed in to GitHub. See GitHub’s privacy statement for information about its practices.

We have not added advertising or analytics scripts, custom cookies, or a contact form to this website.

Storage and your choices

Game settings, progress and gallery files remain in the app’s local storage until that storage is removed. Deleting the app normally removes its local data; device backups or restores may retain or restore copies according to your settings.

Images exported to Photos are separate copies and can be deleted in Photos. Removing the app does not delete Apple’s purchase records. You can use the game’s Restore Purchases option to recover eligible purchases through Apple.

For questions about information you have sent us, or to request deletion of your support messages, email Mousaab.khaldi@gmail.com. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, you can use the same contact address. The game itself does not provide account registration, chat or a form that asks children to submit personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game’s features or information practices change. The published policy will show its effective date.

Game support

Need help with Magic Princess: Dress Up, or have a privacy question? Contact Mousaab Khaldi.

Mousaab.khaldi@gmail.com

For a game issue, include your device model, iOS version and a short description of what happened. Please do not send passwords or payment-card details.

Restore a purchase

Use the game’s Restore Purchases option while signed in to the Apple Account used for the original purchase. If eligible content remains locked, email us with the name of the item.

Save artwork to Photos

Choose Save in the game and allow the Photos permission when asked. If saving was denied, check the game’s Photos permission in iOS Settings, then try again. Your in-game gallery and Photos library store separate copies.
